What is real and what is simulated. Rain is real Open-Meteo hourly data for Catbalogan. The 48-hour forecast is Open-Meteo's historical-forecast archive, which for this period matches the observed rain, so it behaves like a perfect forecast and the lead times are an upper bound. Plant readings are simulated from that rain, anchored to CWD's 2022 Water Safety Plan thresholds. The Caramayon outage window (4–7 Jul) is an assumption based on the Tribune report of 7 July 2026. The alarm is already on when the seeded data starts on 1 July, so no lead time is claimed for the 2 July crisis.
